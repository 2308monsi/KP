<s> Malaysia lifts Islamic bank limit Malaysias central bank is to relax restrictions on foreign ownership to encourage Islamic banking . Also on Tuesday the central bank said it expected 6% expansion in 2005 . Malaysia changed the law to allow Islamic banking in 1983 . It has granted licences to three Middle Eastern groups which - along with local players - mean there are eight fully-operational Islamic banking groups in the country . Islamic banks offer services which permit modern banking principles while sticking to Islamic laws ban on the payment of interest . Most of the Malays which make up half the countrys population are Muslims . </s>
